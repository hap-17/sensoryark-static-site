## 🚀 Next Steps: Get Crawled by Google (IMMEDIATE)

### Step 1: Submit Your Sitemap to Google Search Console

1. Go to **Google Search Console**: https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Click **"Add property"** → Select **"URL prefix"** → Enter: `https://sensoryark.com`
3. Verify ownership (choose "HTML tag" method):
   - Google will give you a meta tag to add to your HTML
   - Add it to your `<head>` section in index.html
   - Click "Verify"
4. Once verified, go to **"Sitemaps"** (left menu) → **"Add/test sitemap"**
5. Enter: `https://sensoryark.com/sitemap.xml`
6. Click **"Submit"** ✓

### Step 2: Request URL Inspection (Fast-Track Google Crawling)

After submitting sitemap:
1. In Google Search Console, use the **"URL Inspection"** tool (top search bar)
2. Enter: `https://sensoryark.com`
3. Click **"Request indexing"** button
4. Repeat for each section:
   - `https://sensoryark.com/#welcome`
   - `https://sensoryark.com/#what-is-ot`
   - `https://sensoryark.com/#services`
   - `https://sensoryark.com/#contact`

**⏱️ Timeline**: Google usually crawls within **24-72 hours** after you request indexing.

### Step 3: Set Up Bing Webmaster Tools (Bonus - Free Traffic)

1. Go to **Bing Webmaster Tools**: https://www.bing.com/webmasters/
2. Add your site and verify
3. Submit your sitemap.xml
4. Bing often crawls FASTER than Google (sometimes same day)
