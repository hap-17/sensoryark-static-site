# 11 - LONG-TERM STRATEGY

## Phase 2: Content Improvements for Better Rankings

Do these after your site is indexed (Month 2+).

---

## Why Content Matters

Google has indexed you. Now rankings depend on:
- ✅ Content quality (your biggest opportunity)
- ✅ Backlinks (votes from other sites)
- ✅ User engagement (CTR, time on page)

---

## Content Improvements (Start Week 4-6)

### 1. Expand Service Descriptions

**Current state:** 1-2 sentence descriptions

**Goal:** 2-3 paragraph descriptions

**Why:** 
- Helps Google understand your services
- Better for users (more info)
- Ranks for more keywords

**Action:**
```
For each service:
- Add current symptoms/issues it treats
- Add how you treat it
- Add expected outcomes
```

**Example expansion:**

BEFORE:
"Sensory Processing & Regulation: Supporting calmness, 
attention, and body awareness through sensory-based activities."

AFTER:
"Sensory Processing & Regulation: Many children struggle with how 
their body processes sensory information—touch, sound, movement, 
and sight. This can lead to oversensitivity (avoiding activities) 
or undersensitivity (seeking intense input). Through play-based 
sensory activities, we help children develop better regulation 
skills. Results include improved focus in school, better self-control, 
and more confidence in new situations."

---

### 2. Add Staff/Therapist Bios

**Current state:** No team bios

**Goal:** 2-3 staff bios with credentials

**Why:**
- Builds trust (people connect with people)
- Better local SEO
- Helps patients choose therapist

**What to include:**
- Photo (smiling, professional)
- Name & credentials (OT Reg. ONT., BCBA, etc.)
- 2-3 sentence bio (personality, philosophy)
- Specialties (what they're best at)

---

### 3. Add Testimonials/Reviews

**Current state:** No reviews

**Goal:** 5-10 testimonials

**Why:**
- Major trust signal
- Helps CTR (people click tested resources)
- Improves rankings
- Converts visitors to clients

**Get testimonials from:**
- Current/past clients
- Parents of children served
- Schools/educators you work with

**What they should say:**
- What problem child had
- How your service helped
- Specific improvement (concrete, not vague)
- Recommend to others

**Example:**
"My daughter couldn't hold a pencil properly. After 8 weeks with 
Sensory Ark, she's writing full sentences confidently. Highly 
recommend!" - Sarah, Parent

---

### 4. Create a Blog (Optional but Powerful)

**Frequency:** 1-2 posts per month

**Topics ideas:**
- Fine motor development milestones
- Sensory processing at home
- Handwriting tips for parents
- Back-to-school preparation
- Screen time effects on development

**Why:**
- Each post = new page to rank
- Attracts organic traffic
- Positions you as expert
- Gives people reason to link to you

**Length:** 500-1000 words per post

---

### 5. Add FAQ Section

**Current state:** No FAQs

**Goal:** 10-15 common questions answered

**Why:**
- Ranks for question keywords
- Helps users make decisions
- Reduces inquiry volume (prequalifies)

**Common questions:**
- "What age do you serve?"
- "How long is therapy?"
- "How often does child attend?"
- "What's your pricing?"
- "Do you accept insurance?"
- "How long before I see results?"

---

## Backlink Strategy (Month 2+)

### Local Backlinks (Easiest)

**Get your site linked from:**
- Google My Business (set this up!)
- Yelp business page
- Local directories
- School resource pages
- Pediatrician offices
- Daycare centers
- Other therapists

**How:**
- Ask: "Can you add me to your resources?"
- Most will say yes
- Each link = ranking boost

## What NOT to Do

❌ Don't keyword stuff (Google penalizes)
❌ Don't copy competitor content (plagiarism)
❌ Don't buy fake testimonials
❌ Don't buy backlinks (Google penalizes)
❌ Don't change site frequently (confuses Google)

✅ Write naturally
✅ Provide real value
✅ Get real testimonials
✅ Build real relationships for links
✅ Update content regularly
