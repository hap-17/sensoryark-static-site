# 09 - MONITORING CHECKLIST

## What to Check and When

Track progress with this systematic checklist.

---

## Weekly Monitoring (Every Monday)

### Week 1-2: Initial Indexing

**In Google Search Console:**

- [ ] Coverage tab: Pages indexed count increasing?
- [ ] Mobile Usability: Any new issues?
- [ ] Core Web Vitals: All green?

**Manual checks:**

- [ ] `site:sensoryark.com` search: More results than last week?
- [ ] Site loads fast? (test on phone too)
- [ ] All links working?

---

### Week 3-4: Search Visibility

**In Google Search Console:**

- [ ] Performance tab: Impressions appeared?
- [ ] Performance tab: Clicks showing?
- [ ] Performance tab: Positions visible?
- [ ] Any new errors in Coverage?

**Goals for week 4:**
- ✅ 100+ impressions
- ✅ 5+ clicks
- ✅ Positions 50-100

---

### Week 5-8: Ranking Improvement

**In Google Search Console:**

- [ ] Impressions: Trending up?
- [ ] Clicks: Trending up?
- [ ] Position: Getting better (lower)?
- [ ] CTR: Increasing?

**Goals for week 8:**
- ✅ 500+ impressions
- ✅ 20+ clicks
- ✅ Positions 30-50

---

## Monthly Monitoring (1st of each month)

### Content Review

- [ ] Services page: Still accurate?
- [ ] Contact info: Still correct?
- [ ] Links: All working?
- [ ] Images: Loading properly?

### SEO Health Check

- [ ] `site:sensoryark.com`: All 5 pages indexed?
- [ ] Search Console Coverage: 0 errors?
- [ ] Mobile Usability: No issues?
- [ ] Core Web Vitals: All good?

### Performance Review

- [ ] Impressions: Trend up or down?
- [ ] Clicks: Good click-through rate?
- [ ] Top keywords: Which pages ranking?
- [ ] Best position: What rank?

### Backlink Check

- [ ] Any new backlinks from local sites?
- [ ] Listed on local directories?
- [ ] Google My Business updated?

---

## Key Metrics to Track

### Impressions

**What it is:** How many times your site appeared in search results

**Normal progression:**
- Week 1-2: 0
- Week 3-4: 50-200
- Month 2: 200-500
- Month 3: 500-1000
- Month 4+: 1000+

**Good sign:** Trending up

---

### Clicks

**What it is:** How many people clicked on your result

**Normal progression:**
- Week 1-2: 0
- Week 3-4: 1-5
- Month 2: 10-30
- Month 3: 30-50
- Month 4+: 50+

**Good sign:** Higher than impressions × CTR (%)

---

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)

**What it is:** Percentage of searchers who clicked

**Target CTR:**
- Normal: 2-3%
- Good: 4-5%
- Excellent: 5%+

**If low:** Your title/description needs work

---

### Average Position

**What it is:** Where you rank (1 = best)

**Normal progression:**
- Week 3-4: 50-100 (just appeared)
- Month 2: 30-50 (improving)
- Month 3: 20-30 (good progress)
- Month 4: 10-20 (strong)
- Month 5+: Under 10 (excellent)

**Good sign:** Lower numbers over time

---

## Red Flags (Take Action)

🚩 **Impressions dropping** - Content might be stale, fix or add to services page

🚩 **No clicks despite impressions** - Title/description not compelling, rewrite

🚩 **Positions getting worse** - Content quality issue or new competitors

🚩 **Coverage errors** - Fix immediately, click error in GSC for details

🚩 **Mobile usability issues** - Site broken on phone, test and fix

## Monthly Reports

### Month 1 Report (Day 30)

**Expected status:**
- ✅ Site indexed
- ✅ Appearing in search (low rank)
- ✅ First impressions (100-500)
- ✅ First clicks (5-20)

**Action if not met:**
- Check: Coverage for errors
- Check: site:sensoryark.com still shows results?
- Wait: Sometimes takes 30-45 days

### Month 2 Report (Day 60)

**Expected status:**
- ✅ 500+ impressions
- ✅ 20-50 clicks
- ✅ Positions improving
- ✅ Rankings visible

**Action if not met:**
- Add: More content to services
- Add: Testimonials/reviews
- Improve: Meta descriptions (better CTR)

### Month 3 Report (Day 90)

**Expected status:**
- ✅ 1000+ impressions
- ✅ 50+ clicks
- ✅ Top 30 for some keywords
- ✅ Regular traffic

**Action if not met:**
- Read: `11-LONG-TERM-STRATEGY.md`
- Add: Blog posts
- Build: Backlinks from local sites

---

## Quick Status Check (5 min)

### Every Monday Morning

1. Open: Google Search Console
2. Click: Performance
3. Check: Impressions trend (up or down?)
4. Check: CTR (2%+ is good)
5. Check: Top position keyword

**Takes 5 minutes, gives you status.**

## Tools to Use

**Google Search Console:** https://search.google.com/search-console/
- Free
- Official
- Most important

- Free (optional)
- See where traffic comes from
- Track conversions

**Rank Tracker:** https://ubersuggest.com/
- Free tier available
- See detailed rankings
- Track position over time
