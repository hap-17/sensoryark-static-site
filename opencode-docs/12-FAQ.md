# 12 - FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

---

## SEO Basics

### Q: What is SEO?
A: SEO = Search Engine Optimization. Making your site appear in Google search results.

### Q: Why is Google important?
A: 91% of online experiences start with Google. If parents can't find you on Google, they can't find you.

### Q: How long until I'm on Google?
A: 2-3 weeks to appear, 3+ months for good rankings.

---

## Technical Questions

### Q: What is robots.txt?
A: A file that tells bots (Google, Bing) what they can/cannot crawl.

### Q: What is a sitemap?
A: A file listing all your pages, helping Google find them.

### Q: What is the difference between robots.txt and sitemap.xml?
A: robots.txt = permission. sitemap.xml = a guide.

### Q: Why was mine blocking Google?
A: Your robots.txt said "Disallow: /" which means "block everything."

---

## Google Search Console

### Q: What is Google Search Console?
A: Free tool from Google for website owners to monitor their site in Google search.

### Q: Why do I need it?
A: To tell Google about your site and see how it's performing.

### Q: Is it free?
A: Yes, completely free.

### Q: Can I use it on my phone?
A: Yes, there's a mobile app. Desktop is better for detailed analysis.

### Q: Who needs access?
A: You, or anyone you authorize.

---

## Indexing Questions

### Q: What does "indexed" mean?
A: Your site is in Google's database and can appear in search results.

### Q: How long until Google crawls my site?
A: Usually 24-72 hours after submission. Can be up to 7 days.

### Q: Why isn't my site indexed yet?
A: (a) Not enough time passed, (b) Google hasn't crawled yet, (c) robots.txt blocking.

### Q: How do I know if I'm indexed?
A: Search: site:sensoryark.com on Google. If results appear, you're indexed.

### Q: Can I speed up indexing?
A: Yes, request indexing in Google Search Console (saves 3-7 days).

## Rankings Questions

### Q: Why am I not ranking #1?
A: Too new, not enough content, competitors have better sites. Takes time.

### Q: When will I rank for my keywords?
A: 4-12 weeks for first positions, 3-6 months for top 10.

### Q: Do I need to optimize every keyword?
A: No, focus on 5-10 main keywords first.

### Q: How do I rank better?
A: Better content, more pages, backlinks, regular updates.

### Q: Is my competition better than me?
A: Probably not. They just started sooner. You'll catch up.

---

## Traffic Questions

### Q: When will I get organic traffic?
A: 2-3 weeks to appear, 4+ weeks to get real traffic.

### Q: How much organic traffic can I expect?
A: Month 1: 10-50 clicks. Month 3: 100+ clicks.

### Q: Why isn't anyone clicking my result?
A: (a) Position too low (beyond top 20), (b) Title/description not compelling.

### Q: How do I get more clicks?
A: Better title, better description, higher ranking.

---

## Content Questions

### Q: Do I need a blog?
A: No, but it helps. Each post = more pages to rank.

### Q: How often should I update?
A: At least monthly. More is better.

### Q: What should I write about?
A: Your services, tips for parents, common questions, success stories.

### Q: How many words per page?
A: At least 300-500 words. More is better for ranking.

---

## Backlink Questions

### Q: What is a backlink?
A: A link from another site to yours. Votes of confidence.

### Q: Where do I get backlinks?
A: Local directories, partner sites, school resource pages, media coverage.

### Q: Can I buy backlinks?
A: No, Google penalizes paid links. Only get natural links.

### Q: How many backlinks do I need?
A: Start with 5-10. More helps, but quality > quantity.

## Mobile Questions

### Q: Does mobile matter?
A: Yes, 60%+ of searches are mobile. Your site needs mobile-friendly.

### Q: Is my site mobile-friendly?
A: Yes. I verified it's responsive.

### Q: How do I test mobile?
A: Open your site on phone browser and check appearance.

### Q: What if I did it wrong?
A: Almost impossible to break anything. Google is forgiving.

### Q: Will this cost me money?
A: Setting up: $0. Maintaining: $0. Optional paid tools: $10-100/month.

### Q: Can I undo these changes?
A: Yes, but they help your site. No need to undo.

### Q: What if nothing happens?
A: (a) Give it more time, (b) Check Google Search Console for errors, (c) Add more content.

### Q: Is this too complicated?
A: No. You just need to:
1. Submit to Google
2. Wait
3. Monitor
That's it.
